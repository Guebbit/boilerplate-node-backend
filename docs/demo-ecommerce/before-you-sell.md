# Before you sell for real

::: danger Every legal text in this demo is an example only
The privacy policy, the cookie banner, the terms of sale and the withdrawal wording in this shop
are examples. **A real shop has them written, or checked, by a lawyer** who knows the countries it
sells to. Nothing on this page is legal advice.
:::

This page is for someone opening a real shop on top of the demo. It lists what the software cannot
do for you: each line names the rule and who has to act. No code. Checked against the sources on
2026-10-09; EU consumer rules moved in 2025 and 2026, so check the date on anything you read here
before relying on it.

## The checklist

| What                                                           | The rule                                                                                                                                                                                                      | Who does it             |
| -------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------- |
| Privacy policy                                                 | GDPR Art. 13–14: tell people what you collect, why, for how long and who gets it. [Regulation (EU) 2016/679](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2016/679/oj)                                                   | You, with a lawyer      |
| Cookie and consent policy, and its banner                      | ePrivacy Art. 5(3) and GDPR consent. The demo measures nothing until the visitor says yes; the wording of the banner is yours                                                                                 | You, with a lawyer      |
| Terms of sale                                                  | Contract terms must be fair and clear. [Directive 93/13/EEC](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/dir/1993/13/oj) and your country's consumer code                                                                   | You, with a lawyer      |
| Information before the contract, and the model withdrawal form | [Directive 2011/83/EU](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/dir/2011/83/oj) Art. 6 and Annex I. The demo mails the model wording; check it for your country                                                          | You, with a lawyer      |
| The withdrawal button                                          | An online "withdraw from the contract" function for distance sales, from 19 June 2026: [Directive (EU) 2023/2673](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/dir/2023/2673/oj). Your country's law sets the details        | You, and your developer |
| Legal guarantee of conformity                                  | Two years for goods, stated to the buyer. [Directive (EU) 2019/771](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/dir/2019/771/oj)                                                                                            | You, with a lawyer      |
| Legal notice and company identity                              | Name, address, contact, company register and VAT number, shown on the site. [Directive 2000/31/EC](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/dir/2000/31/oj) Art. 5                                                       | You                     |
| Record of processing                                           | GDPR Art. 30. The demo's [data protection page](../theory/data-protection.md) is a starting point, not your record                                                                                            | You                     |
| Processor agreements                                           | GDPR Art. 28: one with every provider that handles personal data for you (payments, mail, hosting, analytics)                                                                                                 | You, with each provider |
| Breach procedure                                               | GDPR Art. 33–34: tell the authority within 72 hours. The demo has a [runbook](../theory/data-protection.md#breach-runbook-art-33-72-hours) to adapt                                                           | You                     |
| DPO and impact assessment                                      | GDPR Art. 35 and 37: only if your processing is large-scale or high-risk. Decide it, and write down why                                                                                                       | You, with a lawyer      |
| VAT registration and the one-stop shop                         | Register where you owe VAT. Selling to consumers in other EU countries has its own scheme (OSS): [European Commission](https://taxation-customs.ec.europa.eu/business/vat/oss_en)                             | You, with an accountant |
| E-invoicing                                                    | Some countries require invoices through a state system. Italy: the SdI exchange system, [Agenzia delle Entrate](https://www.agenziaentrate.gov.it)                                                            | You, with an accountant |
| Price and discount display                                     | A price reduction shows the lowest price of the previous 30 days (Omnibus). [Directive (EU) 2019/2161](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/dir/2019/2161/oj)                                                        | You                     |
| Product safety information                                     | Sellers of consumer products in the EU: [Regulation (EU) 2023/988](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2023/988/oj): manufacturer details and safety information on the listing                                 | You, per product        |
| Accessibility                                                  | The European Accessibility Act applies to online shops selling to consumers since 28 June 2025; very small businesses may be exempt. [Directive (EU) 2019/882](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/dir/2019/882/oj) | You, and your developer |
| Marketing consent                                              | Email marketing needs its own consent (ePrivacy Art. 13). The demo sends only mail about an order or an account                                                                                               | You                     |
| Dispute resolution                                             | The EU's online dispute platform closed on 20 July 2025: remove any link to it. Whether you must join a national out-of-court scheme depends on your country                                                  | You, with a lawyer      |

## What the demo already does, and what it does not

- It asks for analytics consent and measures nothing before the answer
  ([what the browser stores and sends](../theory/data-protection.md#before-consent-what-the-browser-stores-and-sends)).
- It mails the model withdrawal information and lets a customer return an order. Goods the customer
  cannot send back for a legal reason, such as made-to-order ones, are marked by the product
  ([the rule](../modules/returns.md#goods-with-no-right-of-withdrawal)).
- It does **not** write your texts, register you anywhere, or decide which of the rows above apply
  to you.
