# Before you sell for real

::: danger Every legal text in this demo is an example only
The privacy policy, the cookie banner, the terms of sale and the withdrawal wording in this shop
are examples. **A real shop has them written, or checked, by a lawyer** who knows the countries it
sells to. Nothing on this page is legal advice.

The shipped Terms and Privacy pages carry **no legal wording**. Each opens with a warning that the
text is missing and a checklist of the sections the real text must cover. Replace the
`static-pages.terms` and `static-pages.privacy` copy in the frontend's `src/locales/en.json` and
`it.json` (or edit it at runtime from the admin dictionary) before any real order, and write the
Privacy page's cookie section.
:::

This page is for someone opening a real shop on top of the demo. It lists what the software cannot
do for you: each line names the rule and who has to act. No code. Checked against the sources on
2026-10-09; EU consumer rules moved in 2025 and 2026, so check the date on anything you read here
before relying on it.

| -------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ | ----------------------- |
| Privacy policy                                                 | GDPR Art. 13–14: tell people what you collect, why, for how long and who gets it. | Cookie and consent policy, and its banner                      | ePrivacy Art. 5(3) and GDPR consent. | Terms of sale                                                  | Contract terms must be fair and clear. [Directive 93/13/EEC](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/dir/1993/13/oj) and your country's consumer code                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                              | You, with a lawyer      |

The demo mails the model wording; check it for your country                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                     | You, with a lawyer      |

| Breach procedure                                               | GDPR Art. 33–34: tell the authority within 72 hours. | E-invoicing                                                    | Some countries require invoices through a state system. | Price and discount display                                     | A price reduction shows the lowest price of the previous 30 days (Omnibus). | Product safety information                                     | Sellers of consumer products in the EU: [Regulation (EU) 2023/988](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2023/988/oj): manufacturer details and safety information on the listing                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                            | You, per product        |
| Accessibility                                                  | The European Accessibility Act applies to online shops selling to consumers since 28 June 2025; very small businesses may be exempt. [Directive (EU) 2019/882](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/dir/2019/882/oj)                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                            | You, and your developer |
| Charging before shipment                                       | Preorders and backorders charge in full at checkout. Card-scheme rules (Visa Core Rules, ID#0002981) say a deposit is not submitted until goods ship, except an advance payment; acquirers differ (Worldline-style terms allow it, Paysafe-style do not). Check your acquirer's terms; if they forbid it, leave the backorder switch off and set no preorder allowance                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                   | You, with your acquirer |
| Purchase hold after repeated cancellations                     | The demo pauses a buyer's checkout after `NODE_PURCHASE_HOLD_CANCEL_COUNT` own cancellations in `NODE_PURCHASE_HOLD_WINDOW_DAYS` days, on legitimate interest (GDPR Art. 6(1)(f)). Say so in the privacy policy (Art. 13(2)(f): the logic, its effect, how to ask a person to review it) and add a line to the terms: it never blocks a withdrawal, a return or an order already placed, and the buyer can contest it. A hold triggered by cancels the law treats as withdrawals is a grey area (BGH VIII ZR 146/15), so decide the count with a lawyer. See [the purchase hold](../modules/orders.md#the-purchase-hold) | You, with a lawyer      |
| Marketing consent                                              | Email marketing needs its own consent (ePrivacy Art. 13). The demo sends only mail about an order or an account                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                          | You                     |
| How people stay signed in: two set-ups, pick one               | A short-lived token plus a refresh cookie, or one random session id in a cookie. Both are standard; the second is simpler for one shop and its own site. Not a legal rule, but changing it later signs everyone out. [Two ways to stay signed in](../theory/sessions.md)                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                 | Your developer          |
| Dispute resolution                                             | The EU's online dispute platform closed on 20 July 2025: remove any link to it. Whether you must join a national out-of-court scheme depends on your country                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                             | You, with a lawyer      |

## What the demo already does, and what it does not

- It asks for analytics consent and measures nothing before the answer
  ([what the browser stores and sends](../theory/data-protection.md#before-consent-what-the-browser-stores-and-sends)).
- It mails the model withdrawal information and lets a customer return an order. Goods the customer
  cannot send back for a legal reason, such as made-to-order ones, are marked by the product
  ([the rule](../modules/returns.md#goods-with-no-right-of-withdrawal)).
- It does **not** write your texts, register you anywhere, or decide which of the rows above apply
  to you.
